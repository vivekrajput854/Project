Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard (Python Project)

Project Overview:
This project performs an end-to-end data analysis on a retail sales dataset to uncover key business insights related to sales performance, profit trends, customer behavior, and regional analysis.
The goal is to transform raw data into actionable insights that can support better decision-making.

Objectives:
Analyze overall sales and profit performance
Identify top-performing products and categories
Detect loss-making products
Understand regional and monthly trends
Create visualizations for better interpretation

Tools & Technologies Used:
Python
Pandas – Data cleaning & manipulation
Matplotlib & Seaborn – Data visualization
Jupyter Notebook – Analysis environment

Dataset:
Superstore Sales Dataset (CSV format)
Contains information on orders, customers, products, sales, and profit

Key Analysis Performed:
Data Cleaning:
Converted date columns into datetime format
Handled missing values
Removed duplicate records

Feature Engineering:
Extracted Year, Month, Month Name from order date

Sales Analysis:
Total Sales calculation
Top 10 products by sales
Region-wise sales performance
Monthly sales trend

Profit Analysis:
Total profit calculation
Profit by category and region
Top profitable products
Identification of loss-making products

Visualizations:
Bar chart of Top 10 Products by Sales
Line chart of Monthly Sales Trend
Bar charts for Profit by Category and Region

Key Insights:
A small number of products contribute significantly to total revenue (Pareto principle)
Sales show clear seasonal trends, indicating demand fluctuations
Some products generate high sales but low or negative profit
Certain regions outperform others in both sales and profit
Loss-making products highlight opportunities for pricing and cost optimization
🚀 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analysis can be used to:

Identify business opportunities
Improve profitability
Support data-driven decision making
